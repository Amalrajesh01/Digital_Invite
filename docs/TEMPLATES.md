# Template specifications

_Generated by `npm run docs:templates` from `src/domain/design/template-specs.ts` and `src/domain/love/templates.ts`. Do not edit by hand._

Every occasion offers **five designs that differ in the ways a guest can see** — how the invitation opens, how it begins, how the story is ordered, which colours and typefaces it uses, and how it moves. `tests/template-catalogue.test.ts` fails the build if two designs of one occasion share an opening, a hero, a visual direction, a colour theme or the same beginning.

## The visual directions

| Direction | What it means | Typography | Movement | Mobile | Accessibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal Tradition** | Rich colour, ornament and ceremony — rooted in Indian celebration. | A display serif for names, small widely-spaced capitals for labels; script only for the ampersand. | Slow, symmetrical reveals; ornament lines draw in; lamp-light, doors and petals. Nothing bounces. | Centred single column; the arch or gateway photograph is sized to the screen so faces are never cropped. | High-contrast ivory/ink pairs; ornament is decorative-only and hidden from screen readers; every opening can be skipped. |
| **Modern Minimal** | Clean, quiet and exact — nothing that does not earn its place. | One serif or grotesque at large sizes, hairline rules, numbered sections in small tracked figures. | Crisp, short fades and line-draws; no decorative motion. | Left-aligned type on a strict grid; the photograph is a full-width band; numerals scale to the viewport. | Largest text of all five directions; colour never carries meaning alone; generous tap targets. |
| **Cinematic Story** | Full-screen photographs and a story told in chapters. | Wide-tracked capitals over photographs, outlined chapter numerals, a serif for the lines of the story. | Slow Ken-Burns on the opening photograph, weighty easing, letterbox bars; kept short so it never feels like a loading screen. | Photograph-first; text sits in the lower third away from faces; chapter numerals shrink but stay legible. | A scrim guarantees text contrast on any photograph; motion stops completely for reduced-motion users. |
| **Floral & Romantic** | Soft colour, hand-drawn leaves and a gentle hand. | A calligraphic script for headings and names, a quiet serif for reading text. | Very soft easing; petals drift once; a flower unfolds on opening. | Oval or arched photograph centred over soft colour; sprigs sit in the corners and never cover text. | Script is used for headings only, never for dates, times or addresses; decorative leaves are aria-hidden. |
| **Playful & Interactive** | Stickers, snapshots and a little surprise at every turn. | A chunky rounded display face, bold labels, hard-edged sticker shadows. | Springy overshoot easing, balloons to pop, a ticket to tear; always one tap, never a precise gesture. | Stacked paper sheets with rounded tops; big thumb-sized buttons; snapshots overlap but stay readable. | Every playful opening is a single large tap target with a plain-button alternative; no timed or precise interactions. |
| **Film & Noir** | Letterboxed scenes, deep shadow and a slow spotlight. | Wide-tracked capitals for titles, a high-contrast serif for names, small caps for credits. | Slow fades, spotlight sweeps and masked reveals; nothing bounces. | Photograph-first; credits sit in the lower third, clear of faces. | A scrim guarantees contrast; the opening can always be skipped; motion stops for reduced-motion users. |
| **Fashion Editorial** | A magazine made for the two of you: oversized type, asymmetry, white space. | A high-contrast display serif at cover scale, a quiet grotesque for captions, running heads in small capitals. | Masked image reveals and staggered headlines; scroll effects stay subtle. | Cover type scales with the viewport; spreads become a single column with the photograph first. | Large type and strong contrast; decorative running heads are hidden from screen readers. |
| **Celestial** | A quiet night sky with the two of you at the centre. | A light serif for names, wide-tracked capitals for labels, thin constellation lines. | A slow star field, soft light sweeps and gentle depth; never flashing. | Centred single column; the star field is a static layer on small screens. | Text always sits on a solid-enough ground; the star field is decorative and hidden from screen readers. |
| **Vintage Film** | A restored romance: sepia, film frames and soft grain. | A warm old-style serif, typewriter details for dates and captions, film-frame numbering. | Slow fades, frame-by-frame title cards and light grain that never touches the photograph. | Photographs sit inside a single frame; grain is a very light overlay. | Grain is faint and removed for reduced motion; captions never rely on the texture for legibility. |
| **Storybook** | A book that turns its pages: chapters, illustrations and a last page. | A friendly serif for the story, a hand script for chapter titles, drop caps. | Page turns and chapter transitions; illustrations draw in once. | One page at a time, photographs inside framed plates. | Every page is plain content in reading order; the page-turn is decoration only. |
| **Watercolour** | Soft washes of colour behind clear, quiet type. | A calligraphic script for names, a light serif for reading, generous leading. | Washes bloom behind photographs once and then stay still. | Washes shrink to the edges so text never sits on a strong colour. | Text is always set on a plain ground; washes are decorative and low-contrast by design. |
| **Architectural** | Stone, shadow and precise geometry. | A restrained serif or grotesque at large sizes, numbered plates, hairline construction lines. | Geometric masks and precise slides; everything lands exactly. | Plates stack; construction lines thin out but keep their alignment. | High-contrast stone and charcoal; no decorative information. |
| **Coastal** | Air, light and a long horizon. | A light serif for names, open-tracked capitals for places, soft sans for details. | Slow wave-like drift and horizon reveals. | Horizontal bands scale with the screen; the horizon sits above the fold. | Light grounds with dark text; motion is slow and stops for reduced-motion users. |

Package compatibility is the same for every design: all three packages can use all of them. The package decides which *pages* appear (see [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md)); for example Essential omits guest management, the wishes wall and the live-day pages, which every design includes from Signature and Luxury upwards. Language: each invitation is written in one language, in whatever language the customer writes it.
