# Stock photography credits

The demonstration invitations (`npm run db:seed`) and the product site use these photographs so the product shows
real, editorial photography — weddings, birthdays, a naming ceremony, an engagement, an anniversary, a conclave, a
summit and a memorial — instead of illustrations. They are **not** hot-linked: the seed reads the files in this
folder and uploads them through the normal Media library pipeline (resized to WebP, EXIF/GPS removed, served from
the app's own storage), exactly as a customer's photographs would be.

* Source: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use,
  including commercially, no permission or attribution required (credit is given here anyway). Only photographs marked
  *free* were used; Unsplash+ (premium) images were excluded.
* Files are resized copies (longest side 1800 px; 2400 px for hero and venue frames). Each is named by its Unsplash
  photo id — open `https://unsplash.com/photos/<id>` to see the original and its photographer's page.
* The photographers' names below are as listed on Unsplash when the files were selected; please re-check the
  photo page if you need them for print or publication.
* **They depict real people.** In the demos they stand in for *fictional* people and events (Meenakshi & Aravind,
  Meera turns thirty, Leadership Conclave 2026, the Thomas Mathew memorial …). Unsplash does not guarantee a model
  release for every photograph. Replace them with the customer's own photographs for any real invitation, and do not
  present them as a real customer's occasion. The sample invitations say so in their footer, and so does the product site.
* For the photographs listed in the second table the photographer is given as their **Unsplash username** (the
  name in their profile address, `unsplash.com/@<username>`).
