# Fonts used in the daily report PDF

`NotoSans-*.ttf` and `NotoSansMalayalam-*.ttf` are Noto Sans and Noto Sans Malayalam from the Noto project
(<https://github.com/notofonts/notofonts.github.io>), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(<https://openfontlicense.org>). They are read at runtime by `src/domain/reports/pdf.ts` and embedded (subset) in each PDF.
